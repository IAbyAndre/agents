Our Studio

Design rooted in purpose

Founded in 2006, Forma is an award-winning architecture studio specialising in residential and commercial properties that stand the test of time.

We believe great architecture is not just about aesthetics — it's about how a space makes people feel, how it responds to its environment, and how it evolves with the people who inhabit it.

Each project begins with listening. We immerse ourselves in context: the light, the landscape, the culture and the client's vision. The result is architecture that feels inevitable.

Our Services

Residential Design

From intimate retreats to grand family homes, we craft residences tailored to how you actually live.

Commercial Projects

Office towers, retail spaces and hospitality venues designed with productivity and human experience at the core.

Master Planning

Holistic urban and site master plans that balance density, ecology and livability for entire districts.

Interior Architecture

Spatially coherent interiors where material, light and proportion are composed with the same rigour as the building.

Property Consulting

Strategic advisory for developers and investors — site assessment, feasibility studies and design due diligence.

Sustainable Design

Passive strategies, biophilic principles and renewable integration — sustainability embedded from concept to completion.
